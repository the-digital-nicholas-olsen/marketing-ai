# Tradeshow dinner

Owner: Kaelyn.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-03 from the Vision tradeshow planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## N2 Venue brief (T-110)

Capacity and budget come from N1. Every venue must have:

- A location very close to the convention center.
- A fully private room with closing doors.
- Monitors and good AV for presentations.

Ask follow-up questions until the AV is clear: number and size of screens, laptop hookup, microphone, who runs it on the night. Compare options in one table (venue, distance, seats, room, AV, minimum spend, deposit) and send to Nicholas and Laarni.

## N8 Menu (T-30)

Per-head food and drink must fit the budget and Sunshine Act reporting. Record the per-head cost in Key Info.

## N11 OneTap check-in (T-1)

Export the registered dinner segment (D5) and import it into OneTap. On the night (L7) someone, usually Kaelyn, stands at the private room entrance, asks each guest's name and checks them in. EJ pulls the data afterwards for the Sunshine Act log (P6).

## Dinner KOLs

Always two KOLs, already attending the show. Invitations and Ted's next steps use [lumenis-kol-comms](../lumenis-kol-comms/SKILL.md) (tradeshow emails).
