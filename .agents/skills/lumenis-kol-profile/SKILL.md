# KOL profile and Meet the Experts

Owner: Ted (content), with Kaelyn for the KOL list.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-04 from the Vision tradeshow content planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## Inputs

- The show's KOL list in the event's Smartsheet workspace (Vision Marketing Events). Kaelyn marks each KOL **confirmed** or **discussing**.
- Booth time slots from the KOL confirmations (K4) or the treatment schedule (E7).
- The [KOL library](../../../assets/kol-library/index.md) and the HubSpot image library.

## Procedure

1. **List confirmed KOLs only.** "Discussing" means not confirmed: leave them off the page, and recheck before launch.
2. **For each KOL, find the existing profile** in the KOL library and their headshot in HubSpot.
3. **Missing bio:** draft one from public sources (practice or institution site, professional directories), in the library's format: long and short versions, sources with the date checked. Add it to the KOL library with a new profile file and an index row, marked unverified until the KOL approves it.
4. **Missing headshot:** ask the KOL for an official one first. Otherwise find a candidate from their practice site, upload it through [lumenis-image-library](../lumenis-image-library/SKILL.md), and record the source and that rights are unverified.
5. **Send new or changed bios to the KOL to approve** before the page goes live (draft the email; the owner sends).
6. **Build the section:** one tile per KOL (headshot, name and credentials); clicking a tile shows the short bio and their booth time slots. Hand it to [lumenis-hubspot-landing-page](../lumenis-hubspot-landing-page/SKILL.md) for the show's one landing page (C7).

## Done when

Every confirmed KOL has a headshot in HubSpot, a bio in the KOL library and their slots on the page, and any new bio is approved or flagged.

## Boundaries

- A public photo or bio is not permission to reuse it; record rights status, never assume it.
- No honoraria, contracts or contact details in the KOL library: it is public.
