# Tradeshow asset check

Owner: Ted.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-03 from the Vision tradeshow planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## Flyer (C1)

- [ ] One flyer for the show (double-sided if it promotes two things), not a second flyer.
- [ ] Tier 1: the dinner invitation leads; 'get a demo at the booth' / Meet the Experts is the secondary call to action.

- [ ] Show name, dates, city and booth number match R1 and R7 Key Info.
- [ ] Treatment sign-up link and any dinner or sponsored session match the show's rows.
- [ ] Every link opens and carries its UTM from [lumenis-utm-links](../lumenis-utm-links/SKILL.md) (D3).
- [ ] Brand system for the product (OptiLIGHT / OptiLIFT).
- [ ] [Ted's clarity gate](../copy-clarity-gate/SKILL.md) passes.
- [ ] Every clinical statement is in the [claims library](../../../assets/claims-reference/index.md).

## Deck (C20)

- [ ] KOL name, credentials, headshot and bio match the KOL library (C19).
- [ ] Before-and-after images are the KOL's own and cleared for use.
- [ ] Claims check as above; no off-label statements.
- [ ] For US shows the deck is the US team's (Ted's), not the BU's.

Report one line per check: pass, fail with the fix, or not applicable.

## Boundaries

- Reports only. Ted approves and distributes.
- Not clinical or regulatory approval.
