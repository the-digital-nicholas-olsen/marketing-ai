# Speaker presentation

Owner: Ted (content).
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-04 from the Vision tradeshow content planning session; not yet run on a live show. Review required.

A Tier 1 show usually has two talks: the dinner presentation and one speaking session of about 45 minutes (a morning breakfast or a lunch, such as a sponsored V Series or Vision Source session). Both follow the same steps.

## C24 Brief (T-60)

For each talk, record in the row's Key Info:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Presenter(s) | From the dinner KOLs (N6) or the sponsorship deliverables (R9) |
| Length | Minutes on the agenda |
| Audience | Optometrists, ophthalmologists, or a subgroup (e.g. Vision Source members) |
| Topic | If decided; otherwise propose two options from the presenter's strengths |
| New or reused | Reused when an existing deck or our existing slides tell the story; new when the topic or presenter needs it |

New deck: go to C17. Reused deck: go to C19.

## C17 Interview (new deck only, T-50)

One interview per presenter (two presenters: usually two interviews). Draft the interview guide from the brief: the story they tell patients, their protocol, results they've seen, objections they hear from peers, and which of their cases have before-and-after images. Record and transcribe the call; [lumenis-librarian](../lumenis-librarian/SKILL.md) files it (C18).

## C20 Build the deck (T-30)

- **New deck:** outline from the transcript in the presenter's own words, then slides. One idea per slide.
- **Reused deck:** start from the current standard deck or our existing slides, never a past show's edited copy.
- **Every deck,** new or reused: current presenter bio slides, their case studies and their own before-and-after images (C19; [lumenis-kol-profile](../lumenis-kol-profile/SKILL.md) fills a missing bio or headshot).
- Check with the deck rows of [lumenis-tradeshow-asset-check](../lumenis-tradeshow-asset-check/SKILL.md).

## C21 Run-through (T-14)

Only for a presenter who hasn't given this deck before; a returning presenter just gets the final deck to review. Book an hour with the presenter and any Lumenis cohost. Send a one-page brief first:

- the three to five talking points that matter most, slide by slide;
- common mistakes to avoid: off-label or unapproved claims, reading the slides, running over time, skipping the call to action;
- timing per section and where questions go.

## C22 Delivery (T-7)

1. Final deck in the show's shared Dropbox folder; link in Key Info.
2. The on-site marketer (usually the content lead or the marketing director) has it and has connected with the KOL beforehand. The KOL, the marketer and any Lumenis cohost all know who is responsible on the day.
3. Present from the marketer's own laptop: thumb drives and venue machines often fail. Use presenter view (notes on the laptop, slides on the screen), turn notifications off, disable sleep and screen lock, and pack the charger.

## Boundaries

- Drafts only; Ted approves every deck and brief.
- Decks, recordings and transcripts live in Dropbox and OneDrive, never in this repository.
- Patient images need documented consent; no new clinical claim without the claims check.
