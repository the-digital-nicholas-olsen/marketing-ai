# Tradeshow sponsorship deck

Owner: Kaelyn.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-03 from the Vision tradeshow planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## R3 Deck (T-165)

1. Pull every option from the show's prospectus or exhibitor portal: name, type, price, what's included, deadline.
2. Group by type: **educational** first (we bring our own speaker and topic, e.g. a Vision Expo V Series breakfast), then physical, digital, other.
3. Next to each, show what we bought last year at this show and what it cost, plus last year's registration package and booth size.
4. One slide per type, then a summary slide: "Repeat last year" vs "Recommended changes", with totals.
5. Save to the project folder. Kaelyn presents to Nicholas and Laarni at R4.

## R9 Deliverables (T-125)

For each approved sponsorship list every deliverable (description copy, KOL name and bio, artwork, specs) with its deadline and owner:

- **US team owns** all promotional artwork for US shows; Ted creates it and works with the KOL on any session PowerPoint.
- **BU owns** only what the BU pays for, and the booth artwork (B7).

Write the deadlines into R9 Key Info and add each one to the owner's rows.

## Boundaries

- Drafts only. Purchases go through the purchase-requisition-form procedure and a person.
- Prospectus PDFs and prices stay in the project folder.
