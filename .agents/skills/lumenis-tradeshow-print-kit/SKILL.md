# Tradeshow print kit

Owner: Kaelyn.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-03 from the Vision tradeshow planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## F2 Print list (T-42 at Tier 1-2, T-25 at Tier 3)

At Tier 1 and Tier 2, print must be at the booth vendor's warehouse (Dircy or RS Displays) by T-30, so the chain is F1 deadline confirmed (T-60), C1 flyer (T-45), F2 list (T-42), F3 quotes (T-40), F4 order (T-37).

Start from last year's print list at this show: brochures, quotes, the show flyer (C1, Tier 1-2), signage. One row per item: quantity, file, vendor. Send to Nicholas and Audrey for approval.

## F4 Mimeo order (T-37 at Tier 1-2, T-21 at Tier 3)

Tier 1 (Dircy) and Tier 2 (RS Displays): ship to the booth vendor's warehouse by the date in F1, and send the tracking to the vendor. Tier 3 (tabletop, no vendor): ship a small kit to the hotel or the attending rep. Record the Mimeo tracking in Key Info, and at Tier 2/3 also send it to the on-site owner from sales (see below). Updated quotes from the Sales Support Manager must be in Mimeo first (F3).

## L3 QR signs (Tier 1: T-1; Tier 2 and Tier 3: T-3)

1. Clinical builds the consent Jotform and sends its QR code. Swap it into our sign template.
2. Make a second sign with the Calendly treatment sign-up QR (D9), so attendees book their own slot and fill every required field.
3. Scan both from the proof.
4. Get them printed and set out:
   - **Tier 1** (a marketer is on site): print one page each at the hotel business center or a nearby FedEx the day before, and put them in acrylic stands on the registration desk.
   - **Tier 2 and Tier 3** (marketing usually isn't on site): by T-3, email the print-ready PDF to the on-site owner named on the staffing roster: the booth captain or clinical trainer at Tier 2, or the attending rep or clinical trainer at Tier 3. Include short instructions: print one page each at the hotel business center or a nearby FedEx, and put them in the stanchions or acrylic stands at the booth and registration desk (or on the table at a Tier 3 tabletop). Ask them to reply once the signs are out.

## Tier 2 and Tier 3: no one from marketing unpacks on site

Sales sets out the print materials at Tier 2 and Tier 3 shows, so make the kit easy for them:

- In the F4 tracking email, tell the on-site owner (booth captain at Tier 2, attending rep at Tier 3) what's coming, how many boxes and where it will be delivered.
- Ask Mimeo to label each box with what's inside (brochures, quotes, flyers), so it can be set out without sorting.

## Boundaries

- Drafts and proofs only. Ordering, paying and shipping are done by the owner.
- Vendor logins, prices and addresses stay out of this repository.
