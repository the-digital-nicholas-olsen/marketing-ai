# Webinar production


Reusable draft procedure; subject-owner review required.

1. Resolve division, product, market, audience, learning objective and desired next action.
2. Retrieve the current event record: timezone-qualified schedule, speakers, roles, registration destination, responsible owner and approval status. Missing logistics stay unknown.
3. Write a brief and narrative outline; use ../../../corporate/editorial/storytelling.md and the selected tone guidance. Link evidence for each proposed clinical claim.
4. Draft invitation, registration copy, moderator questions, slide outline, reminders and follow-up only as requested. Use existing page/KBYG/slide skills where applicable.
5. Route scientific/claims content and speaker usage permissions to their owners. Verify dates, names, URLs and consent requirements before release.
6. Record approved logistics and status in the operational system. Keep recording, transcript, final copy and campaign results in the document/work system. Publication and sending follow the explicit task authorization.

Content writer review: narrative and copy. Event manager review: logistics and speakers. Marketing ops review: registration, routing, reminders, reporting. Required named reviewers and lead times remain to be set; no timelines are invented here.
