# Tradeshow staffing

Owner: Kaelyn.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-03 from the Vision tradeshow planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## E1-E2 Roster (T-75)

Ask sales for attendance, the booth captain(s) and the sales boot camp lead; ask the BU, clinical, service and marketing for theirs. Tier 1 shows have two marketers. Keep one roster: name, team, role, dates on site, badge needed, room needed.

## E3 Room block (T-70)

When 10 or more people need rooms, look into a block:

1. Ask our Marriott contact first.
2. If that doesn't work out, submit RFPs on the Marriott website or through BoomPop.

Record the hotel and booking link in Key Info.

## E5 Badge list (T-45)

From the roster, everyone who needs a badge: name, title, email, company. A person enters them in the exhibitor portal; check the portal count matches.

## E6 Internal Know Before You Go (T-30)

Duplicate the last show's BoomPop page and fill it: hotel and booking link, arrival times, huddle room and time (E4), booth number, dress, what to bring. Send early enough for people to book. Add the schedule (E7) and the lead retrieval instructions (B10) when they arrive.

## Boundaries

- Drafts only. A person submits RFPs, registers badges and sends invitations.
- Roster and contact details stay in Smartsheet and BoomPop, not in this repository.
