# Tradeshow staffing

Owner: Kaelyn.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-03 from the Vision tradeshow planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## E1-E2 Roster (T-75)

Ask sales for attendance, the booth captain(s) and the sales boot camp lead; ask the BU, clinical, service and marketing for theirs. Tier 1 shows have two marketers on site. At Tier 2 and Tier 3 shows marketing usually isn't on site, so sales and clinical run the booth or table (Tier 3 is often just the rep(s) and sometimes a clinical trainer). Keep one roster: name, team, role, dates on site, badge needed, room needed. Booth KOLs aren't staff and don't go on the roster.

### Who covers on-site jobs at Tier 2 and Tier 3

With no marketer on site, name an owner for each job on the roster and confirm it with them before E6:

| Job | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Greet each KOL, set them up, introduce them to clinical | Booth captain | Attending rep |
| Move shipments from the hotel and set out print materials | Booth captain or sales | Attending rep |
| Print and set out the consent and Calendly QR signs (L3) | Booth captain or clinical trainer | Attending rep or clinical trainer |
| Bring clinical supplies to the booth (F7) | Clinical trainer | Clinical trainer or rep |
| Keep the front desk or table staffed, including breaks | Booth captain | Attending rep(s) |
| Treatments, and covering each other's breaks | Clinical trainers and trained reps (KOLs don't treat) | Clinical trainer and/or rep (KOLs don't treat) |

KOLs at Tier 2/3 shows only talk with customers. Their booth or table hours come from the lane plan (K1), not from this roster.

## E3 Room block (T-70, Tier 1 only)

Tier 2 books only a sales boot camp room (E4) and attendees book their own rooms; Tier 3 books neither. For Tier 1, when 10 or more people need rooms, look into a block:

1. Ask our Marriott contact first.
2. If that doesn't work out, submit RFPs on the Marriott website or through BoomPop.

Record the hotel and booking link in Key Info.

## E5 Badge list (T-45)

From the roster, everyone who needs a badge: name, title, email, company. A person enters them in the exhibitor portal; check the portal count matches.

## E6 Internal Know Before You Go (T-30)

Duplicate the last show's BoomPop page and fill it: hotel and booking link, arrival times, huddle room and time (E4), booth number, dress, what to bring. Send early enough for people to book. Tier 3 tabletops: a plain email is enough (arrival, table location, schedule, print materials). Add the schedule (E7) and the lead retrieval instructions (B10) when they arrive. At Tier 2/3 shows, include the on-site jobs table above with each owner's name, and the KOL booth or table hours with each KOL's name, so sales knows who to greet and when.

## Boundaries

- Drafts only. A person submits RFPs, registers badges and sends invitations.
- Roster and contact details stay in Smartsheet and BoomPop, not in this repository.
