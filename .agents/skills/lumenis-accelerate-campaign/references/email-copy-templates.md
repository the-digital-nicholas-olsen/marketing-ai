# Copy templates

Bracketed fields are inputs, never sendable text. Adapt the content to the approved division, audience and event; run the copy clarity gate. These are complete writing outlines, not approved product claims or fixed send schedules.

## EM1 — invitation / promo

Subject: You're invited to Accelerate in [City] by Lumenis
Preview: Explore [approved learning focus] and meet [verified faculty/audience] on [dates].

Headline: Accelerate [City]: [plain-language learning focus]
Opening: Join Lumenis [division] at [venue] on [dates] for [accurate description of the program].
Benefits: [Three or four specific learning/experience benefits supported by the agenda. State what the reader can understand or do; no unsupported outcome promises.]
Faculty: [Verified name, credentials and relevant session for each confirmed speaker.]
Details: [Dates, timezone where needed, venue, city and relevant eligibility terms.]
CTA: [Express interest / Request event information — use the actual action, not Register if this is only an interest form.] → [verified landing-page URL]
Closing: [Named team/contact route] + approved footer.

## EM2 — know before you go

Audience: [Approved/registered attendees for this event, with agreed exclusions.]
Subject: Know before you go: Accelerate [City], [dates]
Preview: Your schedule, venue, travel details and event contacts in one place.

Opening: We look forward to welcoming you to [venue] for Accelerate [City].
Your event: [Arrival/check-in, actual start/end, timezone, rooms or explicit room-TBD qualification.]
Agenda: [Confirmed day-by-day highlights.] View the agenda → [URL]
Getting there: [Venue address and map URL; approved travel coordination process.]
Hotel: [Booking link, deadline and who pays, from the current policy.]
What to bring/wear: [Confirmed requirements and dress guidance.]
Questions or changes: [Approved event contact and procedure.]
Optional: Add to calendar → [verified event calendar URL; omit if unavailable].
Closing: Approved footer and subscription controls.

Do not invent breakfast, covered ground transport, flight approvals or a travel-change deadline. Do not send the internal approval-sheet link to prospects.
