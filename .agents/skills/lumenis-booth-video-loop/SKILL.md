# Booth video loop

Owner: Kaelyn, with Laarni approving.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-04 from the Vision tradeshow content planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## B11 Video plan (T-40)

1. Get the monitor count and positions from the booth layout (B3).
2. Draft one line per monitor: video title, length, product, and whether it loops alone or in a playlist. Start from the last Tier 1 show's plan. Patient testimonial videos have drawn compliments.
3. Send the plan to Laarni for approval. She is particular about booth videos, so get her written approval before anything goes to the vendor.

## B12 Deliver (T-30)

1. **Deadline:** the booth vendor's video deadline is about 30 days before the show; confirm it with the artwork timeline (B6) and record it in Key Info.
2. **Captions:** every video is captioned, because booth audio stays off. Burn in captions for any video that lacks them, following the caption proofreading rules in [lumenis-webinar-clips](../lumenis-webinar-clips/SKILL.md), and check them against the audio.
3. **Files:** final files in the show's Dropbox folder, named by monitor (e.g. `monitor-1-optilift-testimonial.mp4`), at the resolution and format the vendor asks for.
4. Share the Dropbox link with the vendor and get confirmation of receipt.

## Boundaries

- Drafts only; the owner sends the files.
- Only videos approved for US use with documented patient consent.
