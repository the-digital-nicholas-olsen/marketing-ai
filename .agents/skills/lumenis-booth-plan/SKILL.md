Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-03 from the Vision tradeshow planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## R6 Location (T-140)

1. Screenshot the floor plan from the exhibitor portal.
2. Circle 2-3 options available at the booth size agreed in R4: high traffic, main aisle first.
3. One line per option: booth number, size, price, why. Send to Laarni, who approves.

## B1 Vendor brief (T-130)

| Booth | Vendor |
|---|---|
| 10x10 | RS Displays |
| Bigger than 10x10 | Dircy |

Send the show name, dates, booth number, size and location as soon as R7 is locked. At B3, share the layout from B2 with the vendor and Kevin (Demo Systems).

## B6 Artwork timeline (T-80)

Record the vendor's artwork due date in Key Info. **Internal due date = vendor date minus 10-14 days**, so there's room for edits. Sprint plan setup uses the vendor date for B8.

## B7 BU artwork request (T-78)

Send the BU every panel's dimensions from the graphics map, the internal due date, and this request: "Please send the original Adobe Illustrator files along with the PDFs, so the vendor can make adjustments and check print quality." Booth artwork is the BU's; all other promotional material for US shows is the US team's.

## Boundaries

- Drafts only. Booth selection, purchases and vendor sign-off are done by a person.
- Vendor prices, logins and contacts stay out of this repository.
