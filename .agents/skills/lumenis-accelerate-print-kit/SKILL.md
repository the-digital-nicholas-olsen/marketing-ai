# Accelerate print kit

Owner: Kaelyn (event logistics).
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-10-29 from the Vision Accelerate planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## Standard kit (per show)

| Item | Qty | Vendor | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agenda poster boards | 2 | Mimeo | One per division schedule (Aesthetics, eye care), each with a QR code to its agenda page |
| Table tents | 10 | Vistaprint | Small folded tents with the schedule graphic and QR codes, scattered on tables |
| Backup consent forms | Per clinical's count | Mimeo | Paper fallback if the clinical tablets fail. Clinical owns the digital form; the on-site QR version is printed at the venue |
| Service certificate vouchers | About 20 | Mimeo | Redeem-by date in the footer, see below |
| Lanyards, pens, journals | 200 each | Usual swag vendor | Use leftovers from the last show first |

Raffle tickets and boxes travel with the service team's supplies; reorder only if they run out. The raffle happens in front of the audience and winners must be present.

## Procedure

### E7 Print planner (T-30)

1. List every item above in the event's print planner with quantity, vendor, file and ship-to.
2. **QR codes:** generate one per agenda page with its own tracking tag from [lumenis-utm-links](../lumenis-utm-links/SKILL.md). Replace the QR codes on the last show's schedule graphics; change nothing else unless the schedule changed. For the 5 x 7 agenda card, follow the [agenda QR card template](../../../templates/accelerate-agenda-qr-card.md).
3. Scan every QR code on the proof with a phone. Each must open this show's agenda page for the right division.

### E8 Service vouchers (T-21)

1. Update the footer's redeem-by date (usually about a month after the show) in the voucher source files kept in the team's OneDrive folder.
2. Draft a short email to the service lead with the proof: "Is this expiry date correct? If so I'll print about 20." Print only after a written yes.

### E9 Order (T-14)

The agenda's links must be final before anything prints (the agenda content can still change; the QR targets can't). Draft the Mimeo and Vistaprint orders, shipping to the venue per E10. Record the Mimeo tracking link in Key Info. A person places the order.

### E10 Shipping (T-14)

Confirm with the hotel in writing: the name boxes are held under, the first and last dates they accept packages, and the per-box handling fee. Consolidate into as few boxes as possible, and order close to the show, since late small requests are common.

## Boundaries

- Drafts and proofs only. Ordering, paying and shipping are done by the owner.
- Vendor logins, prices and addresses stay out of this repository.
