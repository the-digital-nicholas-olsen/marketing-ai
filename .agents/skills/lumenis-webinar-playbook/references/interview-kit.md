# Interview kit (steps C3-C7)

The interview is where the webinar is actually found. The topic agreed with the KOL is usually vague ("your last six months with OptiLIFT"); the real hook, title and story come out of an hour of good questions.

## C3 Audience note (Nicholas and Ted, around T-50)

A 15-30 minute talk, captured as three lines in the project's `_Context/audience.md`:

```
Audience: <who we want watching, e.g. ODs considering their first dry eye device>
Problem: <the problem they have, in their words>
Hero: <the product or approach the webinar shows solving it>
```

The agent can prepare a first draft from the topic and past campaign notes, but the two people decide. No audience note blocks C4.

## C4 Question set (Ted, around T-48)

Draft 12-15 questions in four groups, then cut to what fits an hour:

1. **Their practice and patients** (2-3): who they treat, what changed in the last year.
2. **The problem** (3-4): how the problem shows up, what they tried before, what didn't work.
3. **The approach** (4-5): how they use the hero product or approach, a specific patient case, what surprised them, what they'd tell a colleague starting out.
4. **Proof and limits** (2-3): results they track, who it isn't right for, common objections.

Follow-up prompts on every question: "what did that look like?", "can you give an example?". Ask for specifics a flyer or slide could carry. Pull their background from the [KOL library](../../../../assets/kol-library/index.md) so the questions don't ask what we already know. Save as `Interview/questions.md` in the project folder; Ted reviews before the call.

## C6 File the recording (around T-45)

Download the recording and transcript. Save both to the team's Target Audience Interviews folder and to the project's `Interview/` folder, named `<YYYY-MM-DD> <KOL last name> interview` with the original extensions. Confirm both copies open. Interviews stay in the shared work folder, never in this repository.

## C7 Title and subtitle (around T-44)

1. From the transcript and the audience note, propose five title and subtitle pairs. The **title** goes on the flyer and the hero image; the **subtitle** is the ON24 event name. They must not repeat each other's words.
2. Each title states the reader's problem or outcome plainly (run [Ted's clarity gate](../../copy-clarity-gate/SKILL.md)); no claim in a title that the [claims check](../../lumenis-claims-check/SKILL.md) can't support.
3. Ted picks one. Send operations the title, subtitle, date, confirmed live start and time zone in one message. That message clears the C7 gate.
