# Storytelling

Proposed working method for the content writer to refine.

Start with the audience's concrete problem and the decision the piece should help them make. Build a sequence: situation → difficulty → evidence-backed explanation → practical next step. Match depth to the audience and format. Use the upstream slide-outline skill for slide structure rather than maintaining a second slide procedure.

For every anecdote, quote or outcome, record the source, permission and limits. Do not invent quotations, compress several people into a real-sounding testimonial, or present an illustrative outcome as typical. Keep uncertain mechanisms and marketing claims visibly distinct from approved claims.

Owner review needs: two approved examples for each division, preferred narrative patterns by channel, and rules for patient stories and KOL quotations. Store actual interviews and permissions externally; link approved reference evidence.
