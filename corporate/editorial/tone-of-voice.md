# Tone of voice

Working contribution draft, not approved brand policy. Use the reviewed upstream brand-context and audience-archetypes references as the starting point; this file should hold only approved internal additions or exceptions, not copy them.

For each addition record: audience, division/product scope, channel, rule, preferred example, avoid example, source and approver. Keep clinical claims within the exact approved market/audience scope. A warm or persuasive rewrite must not strengthen efficacy, safety, timing or commitment.

Before approval, the content writer should resolve: preferred practitioner terminology by division; channel-specific formality; trademark/capitalization rules; words to avoid; treatment versus device naming; and permitted calls to action. Until resolved, use the brief's wording and flag uncertainty.
