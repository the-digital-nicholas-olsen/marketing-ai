# Press briefing

Owner: Nicholas.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-04 from the Vision tradeshow content planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## M1 Align with the PR agency (T-30)

Nicholas confirms with the PR agency whether any press is planned for the show, then records each interview in Key Info: editor, outlet, executive, date and time. No interviews: write "no PR this show" and stop.

## M2 Talk tracks and editor briefs (T-7)

For each interview, draft two documents:

**Executive talk track (one page)**
- The outlet, the editor and what they usually cover.
- Three messages to land, in plain language.
- Supporting facts: approved claims only, from the [claims library](../../../assets/claims-reference/index.md), with their qualifications.
- Likely questions with short answers, including the hard ones.
- What not to say: unapproved claims, pricing, forward-looking statements, competitor comments.

**Editor brief** (sent through the PR agency): who they're meeting, the topics we'd like to discuss, and any news or assets available.

Each executive approves their own talk track. Store both in the show's project folder.

## Boundaries

- Drafts only. The PR agency and Nicholas send anything to editors.
- No unreleased news, financials or interview notes in this repository.
