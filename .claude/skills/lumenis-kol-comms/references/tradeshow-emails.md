# Tradeshow KOL messages

Step IDs match the Vision *TEMPLATE - Tradeshow* sheet. Brackets are filled from the show's Key Info.

Recurring brackets:

- **[booth rate]:** the hourly rate in the record. The usual rate for optometrists is $250/hour; ophthalmology shows can differ. Always use the record.
- **[dinner honorarium]:** the amount in N6 Key Info, usually $2,000-$2,500. It covers preparation and the presentation. No travel or expenses: dinner KOLs are already attending the show.
- **[role]:** from K1 Key Info: treating at the booth, or talking with attendees while clinical treats.

| Step | Message | Timing | Sender |
|---|---|---|---|
| K2 | Booth invitation | T-75 | Kaelyn or Nicholas |
| K4 | Booth confirmation (paper trail) | After they accept; reminder a week before | Kaelyn |
| N6 | Dinner invitation | T-85 | Kaelyn |
| N7 | Dinner next steps | After they accept | Ted |
| P7 | Thank-you and invoice | T+3 | Kaelyn |

## K2 Booth invitation

**Subject:** Join the Lumenis booth at [show], [dates]

Hi Dr. [Last name],

[RSM first name] recommended you, and we'd love to have you at the Lumenis booth at [show] in [city], [dates].

We're looking for help on [day(s)] from [time] to [time], [role]. We offer [booth rate] for your time.

If that works for you, just reply here and I'll confirm the details.

Thank you,
[Sender]

*Notes:* don't turn down an RSM's recommendation. Offer the block length the lane plan needs (K1). Avoid overlap with other KOLs.

## K4 Booth confirmation

**Subject:** Confirmed: Lumenis booth at [show], [day]

Hi Dr. [Last name],

Thank you for joining us. To confirm:

- **When:** [day, date], [start]-[end] ([time zone of the venue])
- **Where:** Lumenis booth [booth number], [convention center]
- **What:** [role]
- **Honorarium:** [booth rate] x [hours] = [total]

Please check in at the front desk of the booth when you arrive and we'll get you set up. Reply here with any questions.

Best,
Kaelyn

*Notes:* send as soon as they accept; this email is the paper trail. Send a short reminder about a week before the show.

## N6 Dinner invitation

**Subject:** Invitation to present at the Lumenis dinner at [show]

Hi Dr. [Last name],

We'd be honored to have you present at the Lumenis [optometry / ophthalmology] dinner during [show] in [city].

- **When:** [day, date], [time]
- **Where:** [venue], a short walk from the convention center
- **What:** a dinner presentation, including preparation (deck creation and/or review, before-and-after selection, etc.), with [other KOL]
- **Honorarium:** [dinner honorarium]

If you're able to join us, just reply here and Ted from our team will follow up with next steps.

Thank you for considering it,
Kaelyn

*Notes:* keep it high level, but always name preparation so the KOL agrees to the honorarium in good faith (a past KOL believed deck review time wasn't included). Don't offer travel. Only invite KOLs already attending the show. Always two KOLs per dinner.

## N7 Dinner next steps

**Subject:** Re: Invitation to present at the Lumenis dinner at [show]

Hi Dr. [Last name],

Fantastic, thank you. I'm looking forward to working with you on this.

I'll send a time to meet in the next week. Here's what to expect between now and the dinner:

- [Pre-show interview, about 30 minutes]
- [Deck review (or creation) together, about [weeks] before the show]
- A current headshot and confirmation of your bio
- [Case studies and your own before-and-after images]

Best,
Ted

*Notes:* list only the steps this show needs. Returning KOLs presenting an existing deck may need only the deck review line.

Thank you for joining us at [show]. [One personal line about their time at the booth or their presentation.]

Kaelyn

*Notes:* attach the prefilled invoice (amount, PO number and description from the record) and cc finance as the record says. The amount must match the approved requisition and PO. A handwritten card follows (P8).
