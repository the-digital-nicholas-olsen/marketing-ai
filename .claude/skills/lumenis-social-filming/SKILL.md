# Social filming at a show

Owner: Kaelyn (coordination), with Nicholas for the BU ask.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-04 from the Vision tradeshow content planning session; not yet run. Review required.

The BU sometimes films KOLs at a show for social media, using an outside video team, and often asks late for a room. Asking early lets us set expectations with KOLs and budget for it.

## K5 Ask the BU (T-110, before any KOL is recruited)

Draft one email to the BU marketing contact: is anyone filming at this show, which KOLs or topics do they want, and will they need an off-site room? Record yes or no in Key Info.

If yes: the booth and dinner KOL invitations (K2, N6, through [lumenis-kol-comms](../lumenis-kol-comms/SKILL.md)) add one line saying there may be social media filming at the show, so KOLs agree knowing it.

## K6 Filming room (T-45, if needed)

We usually book it. Near the venue so KOLs can step away from the booth; enough space and quiet for a camera crew. Draft the request and purchase details; the owner books and pays.

## K7 Filming schedule (T-30)

Add a **Social Filming** sheet to the show's event workspace in Smartsheet. One row per KOL: slot, location (booth or off-site room), topic, contact status, BU contact. Slots must not clash with the KOL's booth hours (E7), the dinner or their session. We schedule the KOLs ourselves.

## K8 Hand-off (T-21)

Draft an introduction email per KOL to the BU or its video team with the slot, location and what to prepare. After that, shoot prep is theirs.

## Boundaries

- Drafts only; nothing is booked, paid or sent without the owner.
- KOL schedules and contacts stay in Smartsheet, not in this repository.
