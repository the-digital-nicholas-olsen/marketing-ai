# Treatment lane planner

Owner: Kaelyn, with Nicholas.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-03 from the Vision tradeshow planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## Inputs

- Show hours per day (exhibitor portal), booth layout and lane count from B2.
- Clinical trainers attending (E2), and how long each is willing to treat per day.
- The show's tier and type (R1): optometry or ophthalmology.

## K1 Lane plan (T-100)

1. **Lanes.** With sales and clinical, decide how many lanes are open at once (usually 1-3).
2. **Hours needed** = open lanes x show hours, per day.
3. **Clinical coverage.** Hours each trainer treats per day, minus breaks. Clinical can't treat all day.
4. **KOL hours to fill** = hours needed - clinical coverage. Only LIFT-trained KOLs. No IPL at shows: it needs an enclosed space.
5. **KOL role.** Decide per show and write it in Key Info:
   - **Treat:** multi-lane optometry shows (Vision Expo, Vision Source Exchange), where trainers tire and KOLs can fire the device.
   - **Talk:** ophthalmology shows (AAO), where KOLs cost about $1,000/hour and don't treat in their own practices. Clinical treats; KOLs talk with customers, prospects and leadership (Hannah's recommendation).
6. **Block length** follows the gap: 1-hour blocks for a one-lane show like iDoc; up to 4-hour blocks at Vision Expo or Vision Source Exchange.

Output: one table per day (time slots x lanes, clinical or "KOL needed"), saved to the project folder; totals in K1 Key Info.

## E7 Show and treatment schedule (T-35)

Fill the K1 grid with confirmed KOLs (K4) and add every other item: Meet the Experts, dinner (N6), education sessions, wet labs. Avoid KOL overlap. Send to everyone attending; EJ and Hannah confirm it works for clinical. The Calendly slots (D9) are built from this schedule.

## Example (fictional)

A one-lane show, 8 hours a day for 2 days = 16 hours. One trainer treats 5 hours a day = 10 hours. KOL hours to fill = 6, offered as six 1-hour blocks.
