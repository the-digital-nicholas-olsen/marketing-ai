# Pages

Use the one brand system selected for the piece — Lumenis Corporate, OptiLIGHT or OptiLIFT — from assets/design-system/README.md. This delivery procedure does not grant approval for product claims or make any brand system a default for a division.

## Content and architecture

Registration/interest page: city and date hero with conversion anchor; specific city story; experience benefits; verified faculty; venue and schedule; networking and relevant technology; dedicated division form; approved legal footer. Adapt the actual event span, not just city names. Separate agenda page: section navigation, hero, expectations, day tabs, faculty, social/venue blocks and footer. Unassigned rooms may say ROOM TBD with an explicit qualification if accepted in the brief.

Clone the current approved Accelerate registration or agenda page when possible; search existing agency drafts and published variants for this city first, as the landing page skill describes.

## Build, editor sequence and verification

Build and place every Accelerate page with the [HubSpot landing page skill](../../lumenis-hubspot-landing-page/SKILL.md): one Custom HTML module written in the external output folder, hosted assets only, then its [editor, template and verification lessons](../../lumenis-hubspot-landing-page/references/editor-and-css.md). Each interest page needs its dedicated division form from the [HubSpot form skill](../../lumenis-hubspot-form/SKILL.md); the agenda page has none.

Accelerate checks on top of the general ones: every registration link in EM1–EM3 points to this event's page, no stale city, date or venue copy remains anywhere on the page, and the agenda and registration pages link to each other.
