# Tradeshow print kit

Owner: Kaelyn.
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-11-03 from the Vision tradeshow planning session; not yet run. Review required.

## F2 Print list (T-25)

Start from last year's print list at this show: brochures, quotes, flyers (C1, C4), signage. One row per item: quantity, file, vendor. Send to Nicholas and Audrey for approval.

## F4 Mimeo order (T-21)

Ship to the booth vendor's warehouse by the date in F1. Small shows with no vendor ship to the hotel. Record the Mimeo tracking in Key Info and send it to the vendor. Updated quotes from the Sales Support Manager must be in Mimeo first (F3).

## L3 QR signs (T-1)

1. Clinical builds the consent Jotform and sends its QR code. Swap it into our sign template.
2. Make a second sign with the Calendly treatment sign-up QR (D9), so attendees book their own slot and fill every required field.
3. Scan both from the proof. Print one page each at the hotel business center or a nearby FedEx the day before, and put them in acrylic stands on the registration desk.

## Boundaries

- Drafts and proofs only. Ordering, paying and shipping are done by the owner.
- Vendor logins, prices and addresses stay out of this repository.
