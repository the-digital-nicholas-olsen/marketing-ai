# OptiPLUS do's and don'ts

## Source

"OptiPLUS Dos and Don'ts," dated October 24, 2023. The source says "\*add references" for every item marked with an asterisk. No OptiPLUS indication-for-use or warnings text was among the reviewed sources; get it from the current User Manual.

## What this means for copy

- **OptiPLUS does not treat dry eye on its own.** Dry eye benefits are permitted only when paired with OptiLIGHT: "improve dry eye symptoms (paired with OptiLIGHT)" and "enhance clinical results (paired with OptiLIGHT)," each with a reference.
- **Describe the technology:** dual frequency RF technology, a complementary treatment, first-of-its-kind dual frequency RF technology.
- **Location:** "applied on the skin around the eyes" or "in the peri-orbital area." Do not mention proximity to the eye or treatment on the eyelids.
- **Regulatory status:** "FDA-cleared," never "FDA-approved."

## Do's and don'ts

| Topic | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Technology and positioning | Dual frequency RF technology · Complementary treatment · First of its kind dual frequency RF technology | RF device / technology · Solution · A bright solution for dry eyes |
| Benefits | Improve dry eye symptoms (paired with OptiLIGHT)\* · Enhance clinical results (paired with OptiLIGHT)\* · Provides relief | OptiPLUS treats dry eye · OptiPLUS treats dry eye signs and symptoms |
| RF | Deliver heat across different skin layers\* | Heats the skin |
| Comfort | Minimized pain\* | Zero pain |
| Treatment experience | Gentle | Non-surgical · Spa-like treatment |
| Treatment area | Applied on the skin around the eyes · Applied on the skin in the peri-orbital area | Mentioning proximity to the eye · Mentioning treatment on eyelids |
| Performance | Effective · Quick · Safe | The best treatment · Long-lasting · Long term |
| Pairing | Powerful pairing (with OptiLIGHT or OPT technology)\* | Powerful combination |
| Regulatory status | FDA-cleared | FDA-approved |
| Skin types | Treat a wide range of skin types | Treat dry eye in a wide range of skin types |
| Evidence | Based on clinical study\* | Based on clinical research |
| Aesthetic outcomes | Enhance / promote collagen production\* · Enhance skin rejuvenation\* · Enhance aesthetic results\* | Tighten the skin · Anti-wrinkle · Anti-aging |

\* Needs a reference on the piece. The IPL + RF study is now published: Chelnis J, Garcia CN, Hamza H. Clin Ophthalmol. 2023;17:3089–3102 (see [OP-2](../claims-reference/optiplus.md)).

Note the differences from OptiLIFT: for OptiPLUS, "non-surgical" and "tighten the skin" are don'ts, while both are do's for OptiLIFT. Check each product's own list.
