# Regulatory language and do's and don'ts

This collection records what Lumenis legal and regulatory sources say each Vision product may and may not be called, plus the indication-for-use and warnings text that must travel with it. Check copy against the product file here **before** the [claims library](../claims-reference/index.md): a claim can have a confirmed citation and still be off-label or use a word legal has ruled out.

Nothing here is approval. It is a record of the source documents so drafts start closer to compliant. Every piece still goes through legal and regulatory review.

## How to use it

1. Open the file for every product the piece mentions. A combined piece follows **each** product's rules. Never borrow one product's permitted wording for another: "a bright solution for dry eyes" belongs to OptiLIGHT and is a don't for OptiLIFT and OptiPLUS.
2. Check every product description, benefit line and headline against the do's and don'ts. Where a "do" carries an asterisk, it needs a reference on the piece.
3. Add the product's indication-for-use and warnings text wherever the channel requires it. Combined materials carry both products' text, as the combined OptiLIGHT and OptiLIFT legal title card does.
4. Then check any statistic or study result in the [claims library](../claims-reference/index.md).
5. Record anything the sources do not settle in the product file's "Open questions" section, and ask the regulatory owner. Do not resolve it by guessing.

## Sources

The sources are Lumenis internal working documents: legal do's-and-don'ts lists, legal-language title cards and product quick reference guides. They are summarized and quoted here for drafting, not redistributed. Where a source has a PB number, the file gives it. Word lists without a PB number are identified by title and date. Always confirm indication and warnings text against the current User Manual, because labeling changes between revisions.

## Adding or updating an entry

Quote regulatory text verbatim and give the source and revision. Keep do's and don'ts in the pairs the source uses, so the permitted wording sits next to the wording it replaces. When a source conflicts with another source or with existing Lumenis material, add it to "Open questions" rather than choosing one. Run `node scripts/validate.mjs` before opening a pull request.
