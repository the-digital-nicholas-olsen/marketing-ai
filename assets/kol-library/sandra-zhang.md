# Sandra Zhang, OD

**Current affiliation:** Optometrist, Gemini Optometry, Moreno Valley, CA

## Longer biography

Dr. Sandra Zhang is an optometrist at Gemini Optometry in Moreno Valley, California. She earned her Doctor of Optometry through UC Berkeley and the Southern California College of Optometry as a Regents' and Chancellor's Scholar, and completed her residency at the VA West Los Angeles. Her clinical focus includes LASIK consultations and post-operative care, specialty contact lens fitting, and broad primary care optometry. She is fluent in English, Spanish, and Mandarin Chinese, which supports a diverse patient base in the Moreno Valley/Inland Empire area.
