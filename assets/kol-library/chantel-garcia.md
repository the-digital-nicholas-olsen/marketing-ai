# Chantel N. Garcia, OD

**Current affiliation:** Optometrist, Carolina Eye Doctors, Harrisburg, NC

Her practice site lists her as Dr. Chantel N. Garcia. The NPPES registry lists her as Chantel Nicole Garcia Tarbett, O.D.

## Longer biography

Dr. Chantel Garcia is an optometrist at Carolina Eye Doctors in Harrisburg, North Carolina, where she treats advanced dry eye disease, keratoconus and hard-to-fit contact lens patients, and provides myopia management and cataract co-management. A Miami native, she earned her Doctor of Optometry with honors from Nova Southeastern University College of Optometry. As a student she treated ocular disease at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center, worked in sports medicine with a team of doctors for the University of Miami, and did research with children and adolescents with autism.

She completed a residency in cornea and medical contact lenses at Salus University in Philadelphia, fitting medical contact lenses for infants, keratoconus patients and post-surgical patients. She then joined Eye Doctors of Washington in Washington, DC, working alongside corneal surgeons. Her patients there included White House correspondents and players from the Washington Capitals and Wizards.

She is an associate professor at Salus University and has lectured on corneal disease in the United States, Europe and Asia. She speaks Spanish fluently. Carolina Eye Doctors offers Lumenis OptiLIGHT IPL and NuEra Tight for dry eye, along with TriLift treatments in its aesthetics service.

## Short biography
