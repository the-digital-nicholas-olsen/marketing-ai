# Melynda Torres, OD

**Current affiliation:** Founder, SALT + LIGHT EYE CARE, Albuquerque, NM

Goes by "Dr. Mindy" on her practice site and with patients.

## Longer biography

Dr. Melynda Torres is an optometrist and the founder of SALT + LIGHT EYE CARE, a concierge-style, appointment-only practice in Albuquerque, New Mexico. Born and raised in New Mexico, she earned a degree in biology and psychology at the University of New Mexico and her Doctor of Optometry at the Southern California College of Optometry in 2010. She completed her residency at the Veterans Affairs hospital in Albuquerque, with a focus on ocular disease.

She spent about her first ten years in practice working in other optometrists' offices, and worked with surgeons on pre- and post-operative care. When COVID-19 closures in 2020 halted routine eye care, she began seeing patients for exams in their homes, and from that work she opened her own practice. The office has one exam lane by design, so she sees one patient at a time.

Dry eye is the focus of her clinical work. She developed chronic dry eye herself after LASIK and a move back to New Mexico's dry climate, and now builds individual treatment plans for patients with dry eye and contact lens intolerance. She is a key opinion leader for Lumenis OptiLIGHT IPL and OptiPLUS, and her practice also offers OptiLIFT and TriLift aesthetic treatments.

The practice is faith-based. A portion of every service funds free eye care for underserved patients, the practice provides free exams and glasses for children in foster care or living below the poverty line, and Dr. Torres has volunteered on Citizen Church eye care trips to Kenya, Haiti, Mexico, Ecuador and Thailand.

## Short biography

Dr. Melynda Torres, OD, founded SALT + LIGHT EYE CARE in Albuquerque, New Mexico, where she specializes in dry eye diagnosis and treatment. A graduate of the Southern California College of Optometry with a residency at the Albuquerque VA, she is a Lumenis key opinion leader for OptiLIGHT IPL and OptiPLUS.
