# Clare Halleran, OD, FAAO

**Current affiliation:** Owner, East Coast Eye Care, Clarenville, NL, Canada

## Longer biography

Dr. Clare Halleran is an optometrist and the owner of East Coast Eye Care in Clarenville, Newfoundland and Labrador, a family-run practice founded in 1979 by Drs. Dean and Debora Halleran. She graduated from the Southern California College of Optometry and practised in Virginia, Arizona and Utah before returning to Clarenville to join the practice in 2011. She took ownership at the end of 2016.

Dr. Halleran lectures nationally to optometrists on the treatment of dry eye disease, and teaches and lectures at Memorial University's School of Pharmacy. She takes part in clinical research and has presented research posters at international conferences. She is a Fellow of the American Academy of Optometry, and has discussed myopia management in a rural practice in an interview with *Contact Lens Spectrum* (2023).

## Short biography
