## Notes and flags

- **High-priority:** OG-4 and OG-5 are the clearest catch in this pass — both point to a URL whose own highlighted text says "72 percent," not the 81%/80% shown on the slide. This should be checked and corrected (or re-sourced) before the deck's next revision, independent of anything else in this library.
- **OG-1:** the "Americans with dry eye" figure genuinely differs between the current trifold (30-40M) and the deck (49M); these are not just rounding of the same number.
- **OG-12:** the 6.3x gland-count figure is the one quantitative claim in this pass that most clearly doesn't reconcile with the identified source paper's headline numbers (4.7x by this review's calculation from that paper's reported values) — worth a direct check against the paper's full tables (which may report the 6.3x figure for a specific lid or gland subset) before the next reprint.
- Several `needs review` entries above (OG-2, OG-3, OG-6, OG-9, OG-10, OG-11) reflect that this pass confirmed the cited source exists and is on-topic, but did not re-derive every individual number from the primary source's full text/tables — that deeper check is still worth doing before treating those specific figures as fully verified, especially before any new external use.
