## Notes and flags

- **Product-evidence naming:** TL-8 uses a real published DMSt study, but that does not by itself settle whether the exact study and indication can be promoted under the triLIFT for Eye Care name in every market.
- **Anatomy and protocol transfer:** TL-4 to TL-7 rely on studies with other devices, treatment schedules or anatomy. The values may be useful background evidence, but should never be presented as if measured in the brochure's exact treatment.
- **Shared unresolved statistics:** TL-2 and TL-3 repeat citation problems already visible in OptiLIFT materials; repeating a number across brands does not independently corroborate it.
