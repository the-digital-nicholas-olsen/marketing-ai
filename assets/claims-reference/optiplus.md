## Notes and flags

- **OP-2/OP-3 citation transfer:** the existence of a real publication is not enough when the route of administration, anatomy, device, or endpoint differs. The oral-collagen review should not be presented as direct proof of RF-induced collagen production.
- **OP-4 denominator problem:** "49% thicker dermis" sounds like a robust cohort outcome when detached from context; in the source, the histopathology comparison is from one abdominal subject. Always carry that qualification with the number.
- **OP-1 study design:** the combined protocol improved several outcomes, but the study cannot isolate the incremental effect of RF from IPL/MGX through a randomized concurrent comparison.
