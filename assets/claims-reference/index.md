## Highest-priority flags

These are worth a human/regulatory look before the next round of material revisions, independent of the rest of the library:

1. **OptiLIGHT deck, slide 4 (OG-4, OG-5):** two statistics (81% frustrated, 80% want more effective treatment) cite a source URL whose own highlighted text reads "72 percent" — likely a citation error, not just an unconfirmed source.
2. **OptiLIGHT trifold, p.2 (OG-12):** the "6.3x more expressible glands" figure does not match the headline numbers in its likely source paper (which computes to about 4.7x); the "2.7x tear breakup time" figure in the same claim does match.
3. **OptiLIFT deck + brochure (OL-1) and OptiLIGHT deck + trifold (OG-1):** each product has two current materials stating different numbers for what reads as the same underlying claim (muscle loss per decade; Americans with dry eye disease).
4. **OptiLIFT (OL-8):** good news — the deck's "data on file, manuscript under preparation" study has since been published (Chelnis & Chelnis, Clin Ophthalmol 2025) and its published figures support the deck's claims. The deck's citation should be updated to the published reference.
5. **OptiLIFT/triLIFT "2.2-fold" claim (OL-4, TL-2):** cited to Doan et al. 2020 in current materials, but this review could not locate that figure in the paper; do not treat it as verified.
6. **OptiLIGHT study-count escalation (OG-8):** reviewed materials variously say "over 40," "over 50," and "over 80" studies, while the clinical-studies book's index lists 74 entries. These are date- and inclusion-criteria-sensitive claims and need a dated methodology.
7. **OptiPLUS evidence transfer (OP-2 to OP-4) and triLIFT evidence transfer (TL-5 to TL-8):** several valid publications concern oral collagen, abdominal skin, another device/protocol, or broader skeletal/facial muscle—not the exact marketed device/anatomy/outcome. The qualifications in the product files are essential.
