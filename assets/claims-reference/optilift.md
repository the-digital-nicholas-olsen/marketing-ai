## Notes and flags

- **OL-N1 (3-8%/decade citation conflict):** Two OptiLIFT materials state the identical "3-8% per decade" figure but cite two unrelated sources — a general sarcopenia review (deck) and a dry-eye epidemiology site (brochure) whose retrievable content doesn't contain the stat. Recommend Ted/Nicholas confirm a single correct source, and consider softening the "muscles beneath our eyes" framing to match what the general-sarcopenia source actually supports, or find a periorbital-specific source.
- **OL-N2 (2.2-fold risk figure):** Both OptiLIFT materials (and, per the OptiLIGHT trifold reviewed alongside these, that product too) reuse a "2.2x" risk figure pointing to the same Doan et al. 2020 paper. This could not be confirmed from the paper's retrievable content in this review (PubMed's full text sat behind an automated-access check). Worth a direct, human check of the published PDF/tables before continuing to use this figure.
- **OL-N3 (eyelid surgery market size — conflicting/overlapping text):** The underlying slide 8 content contains both a "$3.98B in 2023" figure and a "7.1% CAGR by 2032" figure, with visibly overlapping title/subtitle text suggesting the slide wasn't fully cleaned up after an edit. Neither number is sourced. Recommend confirming intent and adding a citation, or removing the unsourced figure.
- **OL-N4 (superseded citation):** OL-8's underlying study has moved from "data on file" to a peer-reviewed publication since the deck was produced. This is good news for claim strength, but it means the deck (PB-00087230, Rev A) should be revised to cite the published paper rather than "manuscript under preparation," and any sales/legal review that previously treated this as unpublished data should be aware it's now public.
