# UltraPulse Alpha

Module for the Lumenis Aesthetics system. How products fit: [products README](../README.md). Source: *UltraPulse Alpha Brand Guidelines* (page numbers below) and the Lumenis Brand Guidelines 2026, p. 43.

## Name

- The guide writes **ULTRApulseAlpha** as one word (pp. 4–46); the logo reads "ULTRApulse" followed by the α glyph. The guide gives no trademark rule; follow the brief or legal review before adding ™ or ®.

## Colors (theme `ultrapulse-alpha`)

| Color | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Purple | `#BC9AFF` | Main color; highlight or background; the field on UltraPulse Alpha pages |
| Orchid | `#D690FF` | Second color (`--product-accent`): the second line of the double wave only |
| Lumenis Black / White | `#000000` / `#FFFFFF` | The base system |

Both purples are too light for small type on white, so small colored type takes ink. Keep this purple away from the Aesthetics opening and closing purple block (`#7079BA`): never on the same page.

- **Clear space:** 0.5X on all sides, X being the height of the α (p. 7).
- **Minimum size:** 100 px wide on screen, 1.6 cm in print (p. 8). The page's diagram gives a 12 px hero-L height instead; use the larger of the two.
- **Don't** alter or fill the α, outline or stretch the wordmark, add effects or change its font (p. 10).
- The source package's "black & white" file (black wordmark, white α) matches nothing in the guide and isn't included.

## Motif

The alpha wave, drawn from the α shape; a second line in Orchid where emphasis is needed (the double wave); 3.5–4 pt (pp. 30–37). Don't place it above or below text, overlap or stack waves, mix stroke weights or fill every wave (p. 38). "Purple Haze" photo retouching (pp. 25–28) is for product campaigns, not division pieces.

## Imagery

Clean beauty images with a natural, refined glam look; always bright; white or black studio backgrounds (pp. 22–23). The guide's sample photos were not licensed; don't reuse them.

## Caveats in the guide

- p. 8 gives two different minimum sizes (text vs. diagram).
- The "Full black" samples on p. 6 still show a purple α.
- The logo files use `#BC9BFD` for the α rather than `#BC9AFF`. The logos are unchanged.
