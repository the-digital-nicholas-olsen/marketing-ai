## What the Aesthetics system keeps

On a product page or section, these stay Lumenis Aesthetics:

- the grid, golden split, Fibonacci spacing and proximity
- the type scale and two text colors, and ABC Arizona (every product guide also uses it)
- the tracker, title, closing line and footer, in the same place as on every other page
- white ground, photos that bleed or sit inset on the grid, 1px rules and flat cards where they help
- one idea per page

## What the product brings

- **Its logo,** on the text side, never on the photo. Choose the file for the ground it sits on from the module's logo table. Keep the guide's clear space and minimum size.
- **Its colors,** in place of the chapter pairing, on that product's page or section only. Set `data-product` to the product's id to switch `field`, `field-ink` and `on-field`; `product-accent` holds the product's second color. Every color is also a named token (`folix-blue`, `trilift-rose-gold` and so on). A product's own colors are allowed on its own page even where the Aesthetics color rules would not allow them (triLift Rose Gold Light, for example). Peach Beige `#DEAA9B` stays out.
- **Its name and trademark,** written as the module says: ™ on first use where the guide asks for it.
- **Its motif, sparingly.** Each guide has a line device (triLift's triangle, Stellar M22's star, SPLENDOR X's X, FoLix's wave, UltraPulse Alpha's alpha wave). On the product's own signature page (cover, opening, product hero) one device may appear, following the guide's stroke and misuse rules and never crossing text or a face. Content pages keep to plain 1px rules and cards, not the motif.
- **Its imagery notes,** within the Aesthetics photography rules: bright, light backgrounds. A product deck shows the product: the device, its handpieces and treatments in progress, as often as the story needs, beside people. Product shots go on `#F2F2F2` or a light field.

## Endorsement

Every guide pairs the product logo with Lumenis in one of two ways: the product logo in one corner and the Lumenis logo opposite, or the product's "by Lumenis" lockup alone. Inside an Aesthetics piece the footer already carries the Lumenis wordmark, so use the product logo on its own on content pages. Use the "by Lumenis" lockup when the product appears without the Aesthetics frame (a standalone product one-pager, a social post). Never use the lockup at the guide's smallest sizes.

## Several products together

Keep each product on its own page or section with its own colors. On a page that compares or lists products, use the chapter's Aesthetics pairing, set each product logo in its one-color version (black on white), and don't mix product colors on one page.

## Sources and caveats

Each module cites the page numbers of its product's brand guide and the Lumenis Brand Guidelines 2026 (p. 43, the Aesthetics secondary palette). The guides and source logo packages are kept outside this system. Where a guide contradicts itself or its own logo files, the module says so and states the choice made here.
