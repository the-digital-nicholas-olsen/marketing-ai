# FoLix

Module for the Lumenis Aesthetics system. How products fit: [products README](../README.md). Source: *FoLix Brand Guidelines* (page numbers below) and the Lumenis Brand Guidelines 2026, p. 43.

## Name

- Always **FoLix**: capital F, lowercase o, capital L, lowercase ix (p. 9). Add **™** in superscript on first use: FoLix™. It is ™, not ®: the mark is not yet registered (p. 8).

## Colors (`data-product="folix"`)

| Color | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Turquoise | `#00C0AF` | Highlight color (`--product-accent`): highlighted words, small accents |
| Blue | `#2C6C85` | The field on FoLix pages, with white type; small colored type |
| Deep blue | `#073044` | Gradient partner to turquoise |
| Lumenis Black / White / Light Grey | `#000000` / `#FFFFFF` / `#EBEBEB` | The base system |

The guide forbids turquoise fills and large turquoise surfaces (p. 46), so the field is blue, not turquoise. Highlighted words may be white on a turquoise block (pp. 48, 50).

## Logo

| Ground | File |
|---|---|
| White, light field, light photo | `logo/folix-color.svg` (black wordmark, turquoise symbol) (p. 10) |
| Turquoise accent block | `logo/folix-on-turquoise.svg` (black wordmark, white symbol) (p. 10) |
| Blue or black field, dark photo | `logo/folix-white.svg` (p. 10) |
| One-color print or special finishes only | `logo/folix-black.svg` (p. 10) |
| With "by Lumenis" | `logo/folix-by-lumenis-color.svg`, `-white.svg` |

- **Clear space:** 0.5X on all sides, X being the logo height (p. 5).
- **Minimum size:** hero-L height 12 px on screen, 0.2 cm in print; 0.1 cm for the lockup (pp. 6–7).
- **Don't** alter the symbol, recolor the turquoise, outline or stretch the wordmark, add effects or change its font (p. 9).
- The logo files include the ™ glyph.

## Motif

The Natural Movement Wave: exactly three lines per design, 2 pt, each used once; lines may break and needn't reach the edges (pp. 44–45). Never more than three lines, never over a model, never a different thickness (p. 46).

## Imagery

Full, naturally flowing hair; women and men mainly 20–35 with diverse hair types; clean clothes in neutral colors; always bright with light backgrounds (p. 39). Treatment shots are calm and professional (p. 40). Product shots on `#F2F2F2` (p. 41).

## Caveats in the guide

- The hex printed for Deep blue repeats Blue's; `#073044` is calculated from its RGB values (p. 36).
- Turquoise's printed CMYK is not possible for that color; use the HEX.
- The logo files use `#2DBDB6` for the symbol rather than `#00C0AF`. The logos are unchanged.
- "Organic Tone" is named as a brand color but never given a value.
