A vertical (or `direction: 'row'`) group whose gap is a proximity step, so space shows what belongs together.

**You provide.** `gap`: `tight` (8, a point and its supporting line), `near` (13, a label and its text), `follow` (21, a display line and the serif line that completes it), `apart` (34, between groups), `section` (55, a lead line and the body it introduces) or `wide` (89, between stats in a row). Children.

**Rules.** Never space a label farther from its text than from the next group. No lines, rules or boxes: group with space and type size only.
