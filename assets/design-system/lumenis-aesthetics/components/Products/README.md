Every Aesthetics product with a module: its logo on white and on its own field, and its colors as named tokens, each taken from that product's brand guide.

**Products.** triLift 2.0 (`trilift`), Stellar M22 (`stellar-m22`), SPLENDOR X (`splendor-x`), FoLix (`folix`), UltraPulse Alpha (`ultrapulse-alpha`). AcuPulse comes next, once its official logo files and its purple are confirmed.

**Use.** Use a product's colors by name (`folix-blue`, `trilift-rose-gold` and so on) wherever it appears. On the product's own page or section, set `data-product` to its id: `field`, `field-ink`, `on-field` and `product-accent` switch to the product, and grid, type, frame and spacing stay Aesthetics. Logos come through `ProductLogo`; pick the file for the ground from the product's module.

**Rules.** One product's colors per page or section. Never put a logo on a photo. Name, trademark, clear space, minimum size, motif and imagery rules are in each product's module.
