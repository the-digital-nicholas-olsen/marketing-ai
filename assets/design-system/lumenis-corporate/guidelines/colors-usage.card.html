Rules

Corporate materials: black & white lead. A secondary color may differentiate subjects or highlight key information — sparingly, up to ~20% of the format.

Only one secondary color visible at a time. Single-page print: one color. Multi-page print: different colors per page in chromatic harmony. Scrolling web page: up to 3 used separately along the page.

Sub-brand materials: that sub-brand's color can be more present. Never use a color outside its sub-brand, and check legibility on screen.
