The LUMENIS wordmark (clean light sans + a custom slanted serif "L") and the standalone Hero L.

```jsx
<Logo width="160px" />
<Logo tone="white" />
<Logo variant="hero-l" height="64px" />
```

Rules: black or white only — never color the wordmark, add effects, alter spacing/shape, or fill it with imagery. Safety zone = the full height of the wordmark on every side. Minimum size 50px wide on screen, 1.5cm in print. When a trademark notice is needed write LUMENIS™ with a superscript ™. On social posts the wordmark is 2 grid columns wide and sits in a corner.
