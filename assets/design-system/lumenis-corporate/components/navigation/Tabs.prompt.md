Navigation primitives modeled on the Lumenis website concept.

```jsx
<NavBar links={['About','Products','Treatments','Science','Contact us']} cta={<Button size="s">Find a provider</Button>} />
<Tabs items={['News','Events','Insights','Case studies']} value="Insights" onChange={setTab} />
```

Links and tabs are sentence case Arizona Sans Regular. The active tab is black with a hairline underline; inactive tabs are grey. Keep the wordmark's safety zone clear (its own height) around it.
