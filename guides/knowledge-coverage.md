## What this still cannot guarantee

A newcomer has a process, exact source-recorded fields, four email blueprints and troubleshooting lessons. They still need authenticated business-system access, an approved event brief, current design/asset references, sender/subscription decisions, consent requirements, and permission for live actions. No such access or approval is created by cloning this repository.

This is **not a verified start-to-finish campaign automation**. No production campaign was built in this release, and no fresh-person pilot has yet demonstrated completion in both clients. Source-specific page/email generators still contain event data; they have not been imported as a reusable build tool. AI can use the procedures/templates to produce a new event's sources, but a one-command reproduction of the historical artwork is not supplied.

Not yet fully extracted/released: historical reporting/ROI assumptions, HubSpot imports with unresolved consent/notes mapping, print fulfillment eligibility, all product/market regulatory evidence, and the rest of the department's event-specific content history. These are not silently called complete. A separate scoped source review is needed for those methods; uploading their raw inputs would not fix them.

The unhappy-patient interview is correctly filed in the SSD interview library, not uploaded as company policy. A transcript is evidence to review, not an instruction or an approved product claim.
