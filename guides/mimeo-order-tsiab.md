# Order a Tradeshow in a Box (TSIAB) through Mimeo

Owner: Kaelyn Gray (Vision event marketing).
Proposed reviewer: Nicholas Olsen.
Status: draft, written 2026-10-30. The checkout screens were observed on 2026-09-30 and the bundle names in October 2026, so recheck them before relying on them.

This is how to ship one Tradeshow in a Box bundle to one person through Mimeo. The whole order happens in Mimeo, so no planning sheet or other system is needed.

**Applies to:** Vision Mimeo account, for TSIAB bundles only.

**Not for:** a full show print order with many items and quantities.

## What you need

- A Mimeo login. The person signs in; an AI never handles the password.
- Which bundle to send.
- The recipient's name and shipping address.
- The phone and email of the person placing the order.
- The cost center for this order. Cost centers stay in work systems, not in this repository.
- The date the box has to arrive.

If any of these is missing or unclear, stop and ask.

## Rules

1. **Do not submit for someone else.** An AI fills in the checkout and stops. The person placing the order reviews it, ticks Terms and clicks **Confirm & Pay**.
2. **Do not change the saved address book.** Contact details change for this order only.
3. **Do not change the library.** Clicking an item's name opens its editor. If that happens, click **Cancel**. **Save** and **Save As New** change the stored item.

## 1. Find the bundle

1. Sign in at my.mimeo.com and open the **Library**.
2. Open the cart. If anything is left from an earlier session, preserve it and ask the owner how to isolate this order; do not remove unrelated items or submit them. Check recent orders before adding to avoid a duplicate.
3. Select **My Library** in the tree. Search only looks inside the selected folder.
4. Search for `TSIAB` and click **Search**. Pressing Enter does not submit.
5. Read the full names and the **Found In Folder** column. Use current search results; `TSIAB` was a historical folder name and is not a required destination.

| Bundle | Use it for |
|---|---|
| TSIAB for Dry Eye | US, no banner and no tablecloth |
| TSIAB for Dry Eye w/ Banner | US, with a banner |
| TSIAB for Dry Eye w/ Banner and Tablecloth | US, with a banner and tablecloth |
| Canada TSIAB for Dry Eye | Canada |

The names are close, so pick from the full name. Search again each order, because bundles can be added or renamed. If the request could mean more than one bundle, stop and ask.

## 2. Add it to the cart

1. Tick the bundle's row and click **Add to Cart** in the toolbar. It always adds a quantity of 1.
2. Open the cart icon and click **Checkout**. This page is still the editable cart.
3. If you need more than 1, type the quantity and click **Update**, then check that the line **Total** changed. Without **Update**, the quantity reverts.
4. Click **Continue**.

## 3. Special instructions

Add none unless the person placing the order asks for them. Click **Continue**.

## 4. Recipient

1. Search the address book by last name.
2. **If they are in it:** open the entry and check the address against the request. If it does not match, stop and ask.
3. Put the phone and email of the person placing the order in **Daytime Phone** and **Email**. Choose **Update recipient for this order only**, then **Save**.
4. **If they are new:** use **Create New Recipient**, with the same phone and email.
5. Under **View Recipients**, confirm they are the only recipient. Click **Continue**.

## 5. Delivery

1. Click **Click here to change your turnaround time**.
2. Use **4 Additional Business Days** only when it meets the confirmed need-by date; otherwise propose a suitable option and its cost for owner approval.
3. Select a shipping option that meets the confirmed need-by date and approved cost; do not rely on row order.
4. Leave **No signature required** selected.

Check the delivery date against the date the box has to arrive. If it is too late, stop and ask.

## 6. Summary and payment

1. In **Sender Details**, replace the **Telephone** and **Email** with those of the person placing the order. Keep the sender name, country and company as they are.
2. Set **Cost Center** to the one given for this order.
3. Set the requestor **Email** to the person placing the order.
4. Confirm the payment mode is the authorized company method; an existing default is not authorization. Hold handoff if it is unclear.

## 7. Review and submit

An AI stops here and reports:

- The bundle and quantity
- The ship-to name and address
- The turnaround and delivery window
- Where the contact details were placed
- The cost center and payment mode
- The totals table
- The submit deadline in the banner

The person placing the order checks these, ticks **Terms** and clicks **Confirm & Pay**. Do this in the same sitting. Carts do not reliably last overnight, and the delivery date moves later each day the order waits.

## Good and bad examples

Good: "TSIAB for Dry Eye w/ Banner, quantity 1, to Alex Example at the Example Eye Clinic. 4 additional business days, latest shipping, my contact details on this order only, cost center filled in. Stopped before Confirm & Pay for review."

Avoid: "Sent the banner kit to Alex and updated their address book entry." Nobody reviewed the order, the note does not say which bundle went out, and the saved entry changed.

## Notes for the AI

- Use the built-in browser, or another browser the person is signed in to. Wait for them to sign in.
- Never click the library toolbar by screen position. A position click has hit **Add to Cart** by mistake. Click by element reference, and check the cart if a click lands wrong.
- Never tick **Terms** or click **Confirm & Pay**.

A repository merge does not authorize any order in Mimeo.
